[`phone_hint_android`](https://pub.dev/packages/phone_hint_android) helps Flutter developers show a **phone number hint dialog** to users on Android using Google Play Services' **Hint Picker**.

- 🔒 No permissions required  
- 📱 Works with multiple SIMs and account-based numbers  
- 📦 Lightweight and production-ready  

Maintained by [@nikb7](https://github.com/nikb7) and open to community contributions.
